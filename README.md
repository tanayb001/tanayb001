# Tanay Biswas Ph.D.

### Molecular Oncology | Cancer Genomics | Computational Biology | Multi-omics analysis | Cancer Evolution and Molecular Heterogeneity | Data Science

I am a cancer biologist with **extensive wet-lab and computational research experience**, working at the intersection of **molecular oncology, cancer genomics, and multi-omics data analysis**.

I recently completed my Ph.D. in Molecular Oncology at the **Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur**, where I investigated molecular heterogeneity in prostate cancer patients from India using an integrated combination of **experimental cancer biology, genomics, transcriptomics, and computational approaches**.

My research experience spans both **experimental and computational workflows**, including molecular and cellular biology, cancer-related functional assays, next-generation sequencing, WES, RNA-seq, somatic and germline variant analysis, copy-number analysis, mutational signatures, molecular subtyping, and multi-omics integration.

I am particularly interested in understanding how **genomic alterations, transcriptional programs, cellular states/ecosyatems, immunotherapy, tumor heterogeneity, and clonal evolution** contribute to cancer progression, disease aggressiveness, and therapeutic response.
